<p class="standfirst">A skatepark full of bigger kids doing tricks can feel intimidating. Here's how to walk in with a plan.</p>

## What to bring

- A helmet — non-negotiable, every time.
- Wrist guards, and knee/elbow pads for beginners.
- Closed-toe shoes with flat, grippy soles.
- Water, and sunscreen for outdoor parks.

## Safety basics

Learning to fall safely is one of the first things a good instructor teaches — before tricks, before speed. Falling forward onto pads and rolling, rather than catching yourself with an outstretched hand, is the single biggest thing that reduces injury for beginners. This is exactly what we cover in a [free clinic](/programs/free-clinics).
