<p class="standfirst">A skatepark full of bigger kids doing tricks can feel intimidating. Here's how to walk in with a plan.</p>

## What to bring

- A helmet. Every time, no exceptions.
- Wrist guards, especially for bigger kids. Knee and elbow pads if your kid will wear them.
- Closed-toe shoes with flat, grippy soles.
- Water, and sunscreen for outdoor parks.

## Safety basics

Helmet on, every time, even for the first lap around the flat part.

After that the biggest thing is pacing. Ride what you can already do before you try what you can't, and give yourself room. Most beginner injuries come from speed nobody chose rather than from a trick somebody attempted.

For bigger kids we strongly recommend wrist guards. A hand thrown out to catch a fall is how wrists get hurt, and a heavier kid lands harder.
