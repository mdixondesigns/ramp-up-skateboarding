# Operations, safety, and logistics

The practical facts a parent or a hosting partner asks about.

## Group size

| Program type | Kids |
| :--- | :--- |
| Multi-week (Foundations, Intermediate) | 6 minimum, 12 maximum |
| Free clinics | up to 15 |

Free clinics run larger because show rates are lower for free programs.

## Coaching

- Target ratio: **1 coach for every 6 kids.** An assistant coach steps in when
  a program has more than 6.
- **The same coach every week** for a multi-week program. This is a real
  selling point and it is currently written nowhere on the site.

## Where classes happen

Basketball courts, tennis courts, and quiet parking lots. Sometimes indoors.
There is no permanent Ramp Up space.

Note that we rarely teach Foundations at a skatepark, even though the outcome of
Foundations is being ready for one. That is deliberate and worth explaining: skateparks can be intimidating, dangerous, and distracting.

## Weather

If a class is rained out, Ramp Up makes its best effort to add another class to
the tail end of the program. **An extra week is typically built into the
schedule** to absorb cancellations.

This is a genuine differentiator. Most youth programs cancel and keep the
money. It deserves a callout, not a line buried in an FAQ.

## Gear

- Ramp Up provides skateboards at every level, including Intermediate.
- Families supply helmets. Ramp Up has a limited number of spare helmets available for when people forget. The back-ups are not advertised.
- 12-15 boards travel to a session.
- Helmets are required, every session.
- Kids bring closed-toe sneakers and a water bottle. That is the entire list.

By the time kids reach Intermediate, most have their own board. See
`curriculum.md` for how to frame that.

## What a first session covers on safety

Stepping on and off the board is the first skill. Skate Trainers immobilize 
the wheels. Pushing and cruising comes next with an emphasise on a wide, stable stance.

## Registration

Sawyer is the registration platform. Most program pages carry a Sawyer embed.

For programs where Ramp Up does not handle registration (a partner takes
signups themselves, for example a township), the page carries **all the
comparable information a Sawyer listing would have shown** and then links out
to the external registration. These pages are the harder writing job, because
nothing downstream will answer a question the page leaves open.

## Scholarships

Scholarship spots sometimes available. Just ask. There are no forms.

Payment plans are sometimes available as well.

## Unknown

Insurance carrier and coverage amount, whether coaches carry PA child abuse
clearances and FBI fingerprinting, which coaching certification is used, and
host space requirements. See `open-questions.md`. Do not guess at any of these,
they are exactly the claims that must be true.
