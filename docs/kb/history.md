# History

Source material for the About pages. The existing copy on `/about/history` is
in Christine and Mike's own voice and is some of the best writing on the site.
Preserve its tone.

## The origin

Christine and Mike had been married 11 years and in business together longer.
In 2022, looking to shake things up, Christine suggested they "quit our jobs and
open a skate shop in town." The motivation was to get more connected with their
community and take control of their own destiny.

A cursory investigation made it clear that the success rate for
brick-and-mortar skate shops is criminally low.

Around the same time, Mike was running a side project called PA Skateparks,
visiting and cataloguing 60+ skateparks across Pennsylvania. In the process he
noticed two things inside skateboarding that looked sustainable and even
growing: building concrete parks, and teaching kids. Neither of them knows
anything about construction. Youth programming was right in their wheelhouse.

The lightbulb moment was Christine's: maybe they didn't need a physical
skatepark to get started, at least not at first. What if they could prove the
concept out of the back of a van?

## Gaps in the story

The published history stops at the 2022 lightbulb moment and jumps straight to
the growth numbers. Two things are missing and would strengthen it:

- What happened at the first clinic.
- Where it is going next, stated as a specific ambition rather than "our
  ambitions are epic."
