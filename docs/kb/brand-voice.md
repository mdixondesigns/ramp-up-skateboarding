# Brand voice

How everything on this site should sound. Read this before writing any copy.

## The rules

- **No em dashes.** Ever. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- **Third grade reading level.** Short sentences. Common words. One idea per
  sentence.
- **Fun, casual, and reassuring to parents.**
- **Write to one parent, not an audience.** "Your kid," not "your children."
- **No AI tells.** No "delve," no "it's not just X, it's Y," no "in today's
  world," no "whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro," no three-item lists
  in every paragraph, no em dashes standing in for real punctuation.

## Who is reading

A parent of a 5 to 14 year old who has never skated. They arrive with two
fears, usually unspoken:

1. We don't own a skateboard and I don't want to buy one for something they'll
   quit in a week.
2. This looks dangerous.

They are not skaters. Assume no vocabulary. Explain any skate term on first
use, in the sentence, not in a glossary link. See `terminology.md`.

## Specificity beats warmth

A concrete detail does more work than an adjective. Compare:

> A confidence-building experience on age-appropriate equipment.

> It's twelve inches tall, which sounds small until it's your kid standing at
> the top. Then they roll down it. Then they want to do it again.

The second one is the brand.

## Reassurance without overpromising

Parents can smell a sales pitch. Reassure by being specific and by admitting
limits, not by insisting everything is wonderful.

Good: "Some kids land an ollie in a month. Some take six. Both are normal."

Bad: "Every child progresses quickly in our proven program."

## Formatting

- Short paragraphs, two or three sentences.
- Bullets only when the reader will scan or compare. Prose otherwise.
- Headings that say something ("What happens, week by week") rather than label
  something ("Curriculum").
- Sentence case for headings, not Title Case.

## Photo captions and alt text

Alt text describes what is in the photo for someone who cannot see it, plainly
and without selling. The existing alt text on the site is good. Match it.
