# Skateboarding terminology

The audience is parents who have never skated. Every term below needs
explaining on first use, in the sentence, at a third grade reading level.
Never link to a glossary instead of just saying what it means.

The gloss column is a suggested plain-English phrasing, not a fixed wording.

## Moves and skills

| Term | Say it like this |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Push** | Putting one foot down to get the board moving. |
| **Cruise** | Just rolling along with two feet on the board. |
| **Carve** | Turning by shifting weight to one side. |
| **Kick turn** | Lifting the front wheels and pivoting the board around to face a new direction. |
| **Drop in** | Starting at the top of a ramp and rolling down it. The scary-looking one, and a big confidence moment. |
| **Ollie** | The trick where the rider and the board jump into the air together. It looks like the board is stuck to their feet. It unlocks most other tricks, which is why it is the goal of Intermediate. |
| **Bail** | Stepping or jumping off on purpose when something is not going to work. A skill, not a failure. |
| **Goofy / regular** | Which foot goes in front. Right foot forward is goofy, left foot forward is regular. Neither is better. Both are equally common, unlike righties and lefties |

## Obstacles

| Term | Say it like this |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Quarter pipe** | A curved ramp, like one wall of a half pipe. Ours is twelve inches tall. |
| **Kicker** | A small ramp that launches you, like a little takeoff. |
| **Rail** | A bar to ride along or across. Ours sit low on the ground so kids can just roll over them. |
| **Speed bump** | Exactly what it sounds like. A curved bump to ride over. |
| **Limbo stick** | A bar to duck under while rolling. Used in the first lesson. |

## Equipment

| Term | Say it like this |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Deck** | The popsicle-shaped wooden board part. |
| **Trucks** | The metal axles underneath that hold the wheels and let the board turn. |
| **Grip tape** | The sandpapery top surface that keeps shoes from sliding. |
| **Bearings** | The little rings inside the wheels that let them spin. |
| **Skater Trainers** | Small rubber "cages" that lock the wheels so the board cannot roll. Used on day one so kids can practice standing safely. |
| **Complete** | A skateboard sold already put together, as opposed to buying the parts separately. What most families should buy first. |

## Culture

| Term | Say it like this |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Skatepark etiquette** | The unwritten rules at a skatepark. Where to stand, whose turn it is, how not to get in the way. We teach it. |
| **Snaking** | Cutting in front of someone whose turn it was. The main thing kids get yelled at for, and easy to avoid once you know. |
| **Session** | A stretch of time spent skating. Also what we call one class. |
| **Line** | A sequence of tricks or obstacles taken one after another in a run. |

## A note on jargon

Skate vocabulary is part of what makes a kid feel like they belong. Use the
real words and explain them, rather than avoiding them. A parent who reads
"kick turn" and learns what it means has learned something about their kid's
new world, which is a small win for the site.
