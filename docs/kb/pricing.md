# Pricing and registration

## What is published

**Exact price per program, shown on each listing.** No general price range on
the site.

Prices appear on `/lancaster/programs` alongside each currently open program, and
wherever else a specific program is listed. A parent should only ever see the
one number that applies to them.

This means `/lancaster/programs` is a page that gets edited every season rather than
written once. That is the reason the evergreen level explanations live on
separate pages.

## How a program price is built

A program price is Ramp Up's fixed per-skater, per-session fee, multiplied by
the number of sessions, plus whatever the hosting partner adds on top. So two
things move it:

1. **How many weeks the program runs** (4 to 8)
2. **How much the hosting partner adds**

No overall range is recorded here. An earlier one did not reconcile with the
model above, and a stale range is worse than none. The real figures are
confidential and live outside this repo.

## Do not publish

**Never show a per-student or per-class rate.** Ramp Up prices programs from an
internal per-student-per-session unit cost. That figure is confidential and is
deliberately not recorded in this repo. Ask Mike if a task genuinely needs it.

Publishing any per-class figure effectively reveals that unit rate and would
undercut partners who set their own markup on top of it. **Quote whole-program
prices only.**

## Partner commercial model

The specifics vary by partner, but the shape is consistent.

**Ramp Up's fee is a fixed per-skater, per-session rate.** It does not vary by
partner. The figure itself is confidential and is not recorded in this repo.

**Registration usually runs through Sawyer, handled by Ramp Up.** This is the
preferred arrangement and covers most programs. Ramp Up collects, then sends
the partner their share after the first session.

**When a partner insists on running registration themselves,** Ramp Up's fee is
unchanged and is invoiced at the start of the program.

**Partners set their own markup** on top of Ramp Up's rate, charged per skater
per session. The vast majority of programs are calculated on attendance. A few
partners have proposed a flat rental fee instead.

**Ramp Up is open to other arrangements** with enthusiastic partners. This is
worth saying plainly on the partner page. It invites a conversation instead of
presenting a take-it-or-leave-it rate card, which is what a rec director with
an unusual budget situation needs to hear.

## Free clinics

Free to families, with pre-registration required. Space is limited.

**Nobody makes money on a free clinic.** Not Ramp Up, and not the host. Ramp Up
pays for them:

- Assistant instructors are paid
- Every participant counts against the insurance allotment
- Some hosts charge a flat rate for the space

They are marketing, and they work. A meaningful share of free clinic
participants go on to register for a paid program. The specific conversion rate
is an internal business metric and is deliberately not recorded in this repo.

### This answers "what's the catch"

Every parent reading the word "free" wonders what the hook is, and the site
currently says nothing. Saying it plainly is disarming and fits the voice:

> Free clinics cost us money. We pay our coaches, every kid is covered by our
> insurance, and sometimes we pay for the space too. We run them anyway,
> because this is how families meet us.

Do not dress this up as generosity or community giving. The honest version,
that it is marketing and it works, is more trustworthy and more interesting.

## Private lessons

Hour-long sessions for students aged 6 to 14.

**Rates are not published right now** and should not go on the site. The model
is changing, moving from Mike running lessons manually over email to
USSEA-certified instructors posting their own availability and rates on a
booking page. Publishing today's numbers would only have to be retracted.

The current figures are recorded outside this repo. Ask Mike if a task needs
them. See `programs.md` for the product direction.

## Scholarships

**Sometimes** available, not every session. Payment plans are sometimes
available too. Just ask, no forms. Do not add an application process to the
site copy, and never write "held in every session."

## Registration platform

Sawyer handles registration. Most program pages embed a Sawyer widget.

Sawyer's widget builder supports a calendar view and can be pre-filtered by
location, but a single embed does not offer a visitor-facing "filter by town"
toggle. The calendar page currently uses one unified calendar across all
locations. Per-town filtering would mean embedding a separate
location-filtered widget per town.

Where a partner runs registration themselves, the page carries the full
information set and links out. See `operations.md`.
