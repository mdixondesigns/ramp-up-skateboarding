# Pricing and registration

## What is published

**Exact price per program, shown on each listing.** No general price range on
the site.

Prices appear on `/programs` alongside each currently open program, and
wherever else a specific program is listed. A parent should only ever see the
one number that applies to them.

This means `/programs` is a page that gets edited every season rather than
written once. That is the reason the evergreen level explanations live on
separate pages.

## The range, for internal reasoning

Programs run roughly **$104 to $240**. Two things move the number:

1. **How many weeks the program runs** (4 to 8)
2. **How much the hosting partner adds on top of Ramp Up's cost**

## Do not publish

**Ramp Up calculates its fees at about $27 per student per session.**

This figure is internal. It is not advertised externally and must not appear on
the website, in a public document, or in anything shared outside the business.

This is also why per-class pricing is not shown publicly: a per-class figure
effectively reveals the unit rate and would undercut partners who set their own
markup. Quote whole-program prices only.

## Free clinics

Free, with pre-registration required. Space is limited. They are a customer
acquisition cost, not a product.

## Private lessons

A limited number each year. Rate not yet decided for publication. See
`open-questions.md`.

## Scholarships

Held in some sessions. Just ask, no forms. Do not add an application process to
the site copy.

## Registration platform

Sawyer handles registration. Most program pages embed a Sawyer widget.

Sawyer's widget builder supports a calendar view and can be pre-filtered by
location, but a single embed does not offer a visitor-facing "filter by town"
toggle. The calendar page currently uses one unified calendar across all
locations. Per-town filtering would mean embedding a separate
location-filtered widget per town.

Where a partner runs registration themselves, the page carries the full
information set and links out. See `operations.md`.
